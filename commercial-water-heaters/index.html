How to Tell Something Is Wrong

A heater that is starting to struggle rarely hides it for long, and the clues are easy to spot once you know them. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Equipment approaching the end of its expected service life
  • Trouble reaching sanitation temperatures in the kitchen
  • Long waits for hot water at sinks far from the heater
  • A commercial heater that has grown noticeably louder
  • Rust-colored water, leaks at fittings, or moisture under the heater
  • Lukewarm water at the exact time you need the most
  • Fixtures that run too hot one minute and cool the next
  • Energy costs climbing while usage stays flat

Water Heater Care Tips From Our Technicians

A few simple habits, like an annual flush and a quick look at the relief valve, can add years to a tank. Here are our recommendations:

  • Confirm the recirculation loop still delivers hot water quickly to the far fixtures
  • Log water temperatures at key fixtures so drift is caught early
  • Inspect anode rods regularly, since heavy use consumes them faster
  • Do not store supplies against the equipment or block air openings
  • Set a flushing schedule based on usage; busy tanks collect sediment fast
  • Budget for replacement as equipment nears the end of its expected life
  • Verify delivery temperatures at sinks and showers a few times a year
