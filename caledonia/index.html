How Caledonia Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Routine maintenance is the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode inspection every few years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a thermostat set near 120 degrees, and yearly descaling for any tankless unit on hard water, so small issues get caught early.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms like rusty hot water, rumbling during heating, moisture around the base, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one with water on the floor.

The sooner you call about a hot water problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A tank that's just begun to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet that same buildup, ignored through a few more years, can create hot spots on the tank floor and burn out the lower element on an electric model, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off. A quick call saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Common Water Heater Problems in Caledonia

A water heater in Caledonia rarely fails out of nowhere. Watch for rumbling and popping while the tank heats, rust-colored hot water, a sulfur smell from the hot tap, a damp spot under the tank, or hot water that runs out a little sooner each month. These are early signs of sediment buildup, a spent anode rod, a failing element, or corrosion starting at the bottom seam, and when they're caught early, most of them mean a modest repair or a replacement on your schedule. Ignored, they end in a failed tank and water on the floor. Pay close attention to a relief valve that drips or discharges: it means the tank is seeing too much pressure or temperature, and it should be checked promptly. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
